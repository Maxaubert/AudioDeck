Priority order

Rank your gear once. AudioDeck keeps Windows pointed at the best device that is actually switched on.

General notes

  1. The highest device in each list that is online becomes the Windows default. AudioDeck rechecks on every device change.
  2. Click a row to switch to it now. That choice is a manual override and it holds until the device drops off.
  3. Drag a row, or use the arrow controls, to reorder the list.
