How the ranking works

The highest device on the list that is actually plugged in or powered up becomes the Windows default, automatically. Click any row to switch to it right now. Drag a row to change its rank.

Red: in useChosen automatically by rank.
Blue: manual overrideYou pinned this one. Rank is ignored until you release it.

Levels

Each device keeps its own volume. Windows restores the level below the moment that device becomes the default again, so a quiet television never blows out a headset.
